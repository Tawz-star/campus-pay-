      What CampusPay does

      A project of the Fintech Association, Bishop Heber College (Autonomous), Tiruchirappalli — built for about 13,000 students and 2,000 staff across 24 departments.

      Register-number sign-in
      Your 9-digit number is your identity. No separate username.
      Campus wallet
      Top up once, pay at all five outlets without cash or change.
      Round-up savings
      Every payment rounds up to the next ₹10 and the difference lands in a savings pot you can pull back any time.
      QR payments
      Point the camera at the counter code, type the amount, done.
      Spend tracker and budgets
      See where your money goes by shop and by month, and set a monthly cap.
      Digital receipts
      Every payment gets a reference you can pull up later.
      Event registration
      Fest and workshop fees come straight out of the wallet, seats update live.
      Department leaderboard
      Campus points roll up by department, so the ranking is a department contest, not a spending contest.
      Bank linking
      Attach an account or UPI ID for top-ups. Only the last four digits are stored.
      Vendor ratings
      Rate an outlet after paying so the shops get real feedback.
